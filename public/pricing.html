What does the score actually mean?

Each business is meant to get a 0–100 opportunity score for how much an SEO agency could do for it. The signals behind the score will be described when the new data source is live. A score of 45 or higher is banded high, 25 to 44 is usable, and below 25 is low.

Do I have to be in Austin?

No. Tanta Pulse is built for the Austin local SEO market, but feeds are defined by niche and city. Send us your market and we'll tell you what we can build once the new data source is live.
