Common questions

Where do the leads come from?

Public local business listing data for the niche and city you choose. We pull the businesses, score them, and drop the duplicates before anything reaches you.

What does the score actually mean?

Each business gets a 0–100 opportunity score for how much an SEO agency could do for it. Points are added for no website listed, few Google reviews next to the local median, a rating below 4.3, an unclaimed Google profile, a map position past 10, and a listed phone number. A score of 45 or higher is banded high, 25 to 44 is usable, and below 25 is low.

Do I have to be in Austin?

No. Tanta Pulse is built for the Austin local SEO market and that's where our coverage is deepest, but feeds are defined by niche and city. Send us your market and we'll tell you what we can build.

What are the plan prices?

Starter is $49/month, Pro is $149/month, and Agency is $399/month. All three are billed month to month through Stripe.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans are month to month with no annual contract. Cancel and you won't be billed for the next cycle.
