What this page is, and what it is not

If you are a homeowner looking for an HVAC technician, this is not the right page. Tanta Pulse does not take service requests or connect homeowners with contractors.

If you run or work at a marketing or SEO agency and you want HVAC companies as clients, you are in the right place. The leads in an HVAC feed are the HVAC companies themselves: the business name, phone, and website from its public listing, plus a score that estimates how much an agency has to offer it.

Why HVAC companies are a workable niche for agencies

An HVAC company is usually a local service business that depends on being found when someone searches for repair or installation in their city. Its public listing is a large part of how it shows up in map results, which makes the listing itself a useful thing to read before you reach out.

That is the thing Tanta Pulse reads for you. For each HVAC company in your chosen market it records what is visible, and it scores the gaps.

For HVAC, these are the signals most likely to give you a concrete first message:

No website listed

An HVAC company that answers calls but has no site on its listing is giving up the page where a searcher would compare it with competitors. This is one of the two heaviest signals.

Reviews against the local median

Seasonal demand makes reviews a visible differentiator. We compare each company's count with the HVAC median for the same city, so a quiet profile stands out.

Star rating below 4.3

A lower average rating raises the score and gives you an honest reason to talk about reputation.

Unclaimed Google profile

If the listing still invites someone to claim it, the company has not taken control of how it appears on the map.
