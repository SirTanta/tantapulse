What this page is, and what it is not

If you are a homeowner looking for an HVAC technician, this is not the right page. Tanta Pulse does not take service requests or connect homeowners with contractors.

If you run or work at a marketing or SEO agency and you want HVAC companies as clients, you are in the right place. The leads in an HVAC feed are the HVAC companies themselves: the business name and contact details, plus a score that estimates how much an agency has to offer it.

Why HVAC companies are a workable niche for agencies

An HVAC company is usually a local service business that depends on being found when someone searches for repair or installation in their city. That makes how it presents itself online a useful thing to read before you reach out.

That is the screening Tanta Pulse is built to do for you: score the gaps for each HVAC company in your chosen market and give the reasons. Which signals feed the score depends on the new data source, and we will describe them when it is ready.

Austin HVAC as a starting market

Austin is where Tanta Pulse started. An HVAC feed for Austin would be scored against other HVAC companies in that same city, not against a national number. The same approach would apply to HVAC in other cities. Send the market you work in and we will tell you what we can build once the new data source is live.

Using the list

  1. Sort by band and begin with the high-scoring companies.
  2. Read the reasons on each lead before you write to it.
  3. Skip the low band unless you have capacity for volume.
