Prospecting is where local SEO agencies lose time

A local SEO company sells to hundreds of small businesses that each look similar from a distance. The work of choosing who to contact is repetitive: open a map, scan listings, check for a website, count reviews, compare to the neighbors. It is easy to do for ten businesses and miserable for two hundred.

Tanta Pulse automates that screening pass. It does not do outreach for you, and it does not promise a business will buy. It gives your team a ranked starting list for a niche and city you picked, along with what it saw on each listing.

Turn each score reason into an opening

Every lead includes the reasons it scored the way it did. Those reasons map to services a local SEO agency already sells:

  • No website listed points to a site build or a landing-page conversation.
  • Few reviews next to the local median points to review generation and reputation work.
  • Unclaimed Google profile points to profile setup and cleanup.
  • Map position beyond 10 points to local ranking work, since the business is not on the first page of map results for that search.

Your first message can then start from something observed about that business instead of a template that could have gone to anyone.

What you get, and what you do not

You get

  • Business name, phone, and website as listed publicly
  • Rating and review count
  • A 0 to 100 score, a high, usable, or low band, and the reasons behind the score
  • Deduplication, including against businesses already delivered to you in earlier feeds

You do not get

  • Exclusive leads. The data comes from public listings.
  • Verified owner names or email addresses.
  • Outreach, appointment setting, or a promise of any result.
