Prospecting is where local SEO agencies lose time

A local SEO company sells to hundreds of small businesses that each look similar from a distance. The work of choosing who to contact is repetitive: research each business, look for gaps, compare it to the others. It is easy to do for ten businesses and miserable for two hundred.

Tanta Pulse automates that screening pass. It does not do outreach for you, and it does not promise a business will buy. It is built to give your team a ranked starting list for a niche and city you picked, along with the reasons for each ranking.

Turn each score reason into an opening

Every lead is meant to include the reasons it scored the way it did, each one tied to a service a local SEO agency already sells. The specific signals will be described when the new data source is ready.

Your first message can then start from something observed about that business instead of a template that could have gone to anyone.

What you get, and what you do not

You get

  • Business name and the contact details we have for it
  • A 0 to 100 score, a high, usable, or low band, and the reasons behind the score
  • Deduplication, including against businesses already delivered to you in earlier feeds

You do not get

  • Exclusive leads. Businesses are not exclusive to any one customer.
  • Verified owner names or email addresses.
  • Outreach, appointment setting, or a promise of any result.
