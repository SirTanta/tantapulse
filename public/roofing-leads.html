Roofing contractors as agency prospects

Homeowners: Tanta Pulse does not match homeowners with roofers or take quotes. The leads here are the roofing businesses.

Roofing buyers can compare local contractors side by side on a map. A contractor with a thin review history or an incomplete listing is easy to compare unfavorably with the company next to it on the map. That comparison is what Tanta Pulse automates for the agency selling to them.

How a roofing feed is scored

For the city you choose, Tanta Pulse gathers the roofing companies that appear in public map results and checks the visible parts of each listing. Reviews carry the most nuance, since they are measured against the median for roofers in that city rather than a fixed number.

These four tend to matter most when you are screening roofers:

Reviews against the local median

This is one of the two heaviest signals. A roofer with few reviews while competitors in the same city have many reads as the riskier choice to a homeowner comparing them.

No website listed

Without a site on the listing, a roofing company has nowhere to show past work or service areas, and nothing to point a searcher toward.

Map position beyond 10

We record where each roofer appears for the search we ran. Companies beyond the tenth position are not in the first set of results most people look through.

Unclaimed Google profile

The listing still shows the claim prompt, which suggests the owner has not set up or taken over the profile.

Rating and a listed phone number add smaller amounts to the score. The result is a ranked list, with each company banded high, usable, or low and the reasons shown next to it.

Picking a market

Roofing feeds are defined by city, so a suburb can be its own market. Tanta Pulse's home market is the Austin area, and a city such as Round Rock can be requested as a separate feed from Austin itself. The review median is calculated for the city you pick, so a smaller market is judged against its own roofers.

Roofing feed questions

Are these homeowner leads?

No. Each lead is a roofing company listed publicly, not a person who is shopping for a roof.

How do I know the information is current?

The data reflects each company's public listing at the time we collected it. We do not verify phone numbers or websites beyond what the listing shows, so check anything important before you rely on it.

Can I combine roofing with another niche?

Each niche and city is its own feed. Many agencies pair roofing with HVAC since the sales conversation is similar. If you are in the Austin area, the Austin agency page explains how feeds work there.
