Roofing contractors as agency prospects

Homeowners: Tanta Pulse does not match homeowners with roofers or take quotes. The leads here are the roofing businesses.

Roofing buyers compare local contractors side by side, and a contractor with a thin online presence is easy to compare unfavorably with the company next to it. Screening for that gap is what Tanta Pulse is built to do for the agency selling to them.

How a roofing feed is scored

For the city you choose, each roofing company would get a 0 to 100 score measured against the other roofers in that city rather than a fixed number, banded high, usable, or low, with the reasons shown next to it.

Which signals feed the score depends on the new data source, and we will describe them when it is ready.

Picking a market

Roofing feeds are defined by city, so a suburb can be its own market. Tanta Pulse's home market is the Austin area, and a city such as Round Rock can be requested as a separate feed from Austin itself. A smaller market would be judged against its own roofers.

Outside Texas, send the city and we will check what we can build for it once the new data source is live.

Roofing feed questions

Are these homeowner leads?

No. Each lead is a roofing company, not a person who is shopping for a roof.

How do I know the information is current?

Data is a snapshot from when it was collected, and we do not verify phone numbers or websites. Check anything important before you rely on it.

Can I combine roofing with another niche?

Each niche and city is its own feed. Many agencies pair roofing with HVAC since the sales conversation is similar. If you are in the Austin area, the Austin agency page explains how feeds work there.
