A ranked list is different from a scraped list

Most agencies in Austin already know how to find local businesses. Putting a list of names together is the easy part. The slow part is deciding which of them to call first. A plain sheet gives you rows; it does not tell you which of those businesses is under-served online.

Tanta Pulse is built to do the sorting step: remove duplicates and score each business against the gaps an agency can actually sell into, with the reasons behind every score so your first message can reference something real. The service is being rebuilt on a new data source and is not available right now.

What the score is for

Each business is meant to get a 0 to 100 opportunity score, banded high, usable, or low, so a rep can start at the top of the list and stop when the leads stop being worth the call. Which signals feed the score depends on the new data source, and we will describe them when it is ready.

How a feed works in the Austin area

  1. Choose a niche and a market. Austin proper, or a surrounding city such as Round Rock or Cedar Park. One niche in one market is one feed.
  2. Tell us the pair. The free market check is paused while we move to a new data source. Email hello@tantapulse.com to be told when it returns.
  3. Decide whether a recurring feed is worth it. If it is, a feed would be delivered by email once paid plans return. Businesses already sent to you would be left out of later deliveries, so you do not pitch the same lead twice.

An example, not a customer story. Say your agency sells Google Business Profile and web work to service companies. You might pick HVAC in Austin for one feed and dentists in Cedar Park for another. They would be scored and ranked separately.

Questions Austin agencies ask

Are these exclusive leads?

No. Businesses are not exclusive to any one customer. Treat them as ranked prospects, not as leads only you will ever receive.

Will every lead have a phone number and a website?

Not necessarily. Contact details depend on what is available for each business, and a lead says so when something is missing rather than hiding the gap.

Do I have to be in Austin?

No. Tanta Pulse is built around the Austin local SEO market, and feeds are defined by niche and city. If you sell elsewhere, send your market and we will tell you what we can build. There is also a page for local SEO agencies in any city.
