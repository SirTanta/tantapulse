A ranked list is different from a scraped list

Most agencies in Austin already know how to find local businesses. Pulling a few hundred names off a map is the easy part. The slow part is deciding which of them to call first. A scraped sheet gives you rows; it does not tell you which of those businesses is under-served online.

Tanta Pulse does the sorting step. For the niche and city you choose, it collects the local businesses from public Google listings, removes duplicates, and scores each one against the gaps an agency can actually sell into. Every lead in your list carries the reasons behind its score, so your first message can reference something real about that business.

What the score looks at

Each business gets a 0 to 100 opportunity score built from what is visible on its public listing. The signals that carry the most weight:

No website listed

A business with no website on its listing is the clearest web-build or local SEO opening. It is one of the two heaviest signals in the score.

Reviews against the local median

Review count is compared with the median for that niche and city, so a crowded market still surfaces the businesses that are thin next to their neighbors.

Unclaimed Google profile

If the listing still shows the claim-this-business prompt, the owner has not taken control of it.

Map position beyond 10

Businesses ranking past the first page of map results for the search we ran score higher, because there is more visibility to win.

Rating and a listed phone number add smaller amounts. Businesses are banded high, usable, or low so a rep can start at the top of the list and stop when the leads stop being worth the call.

Questions Austin agencies ask

Are these exclusive leads?

No. They come from public Google listings, so other people can see the same businesses. Treat them as researched prospects that are ranked for you, not as leads only you will ever receive.

Will every lead have a phone number and a website?

No. Each lead shows the phone and website from its listing. If a listing has neither, the lead says so rather than hiding the gap, and a missing website is itself one of the signals the score rewards.

Do I have to be in Austin?

No. Tanta Pulse is built around the Austin local SEO market, and feeds are defined by niche and city. If you sell elsewhere, send your market and we will tell you what we can build. There is also a page for local SEO agencies in any city.
